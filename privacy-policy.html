1. Scope of Data Stewardship

This Institutional Privacy Policy details the data stewardship principles, digital security protocols, and client confidentiality frameworks enforced by Overcoat Prism Bespoke Outerwear LLC across our official digital domain. We understand that patrons commissioning bespoke tailored outerwear expect complete discretion regarding their anatomical measurements, salon fitting schedules, and private sartorial preferences. This document transparently explains what minimal data we collect, how it is safeguarded, and your rights under United States privacy legislation.

2. Information Collection

When you visit our digital platform, request fabric swatches, or reserve a private fitting session at our Chicago Magnificent Mile atelier, we may collect personal identifying records including your full name, telephone number, verified email address, and general outerwear preferences. We never collect sensitive financial account details or biometric telemetry across this web interface. All collected coordinates are used solely to fulfill your fitting reservations and facilitate direct communication with our master tailoring staff.

3. Technical Telemetry

Our web servers automatically record non-identifying technical telemetry to preserve server security, optimize computational efficiency, and identify navigational bottlenecks across mobile and desktop environments. This telemetry comprises internet protocol addresses, browser user agent strings, referring domain sources, operating system versions, and timestamp logs of viewed outerwear pages. This diagnostic data is aggregated without connecting telemetry records to individual client names or physical residential addresses, ensuring your private browsing remains confidential.

4. Minimal Session Cookies

Overcoat Prism deploys minimal, strictly essential session cookies to enable smooth digital navigation, preserve fitting reservation parameters as you advance through our interactive stepper, and verify security tokens during form transmissions. We do not deploy invasive third-party cross-domain behavioral tracking cookies, commercial advertising beacons, or consumer profiling pixels. Any analytical telemetry utilized on our domain is anonymized to assess overall site load performance and resolve technical interface rendering errors across various devices.

5. Encryption & Security Protocols

We enforce robust administrative, architectural, and cryptographic countermeasures to protect all submitted personal coordinates from unauthorized interception, illicit data harvesting, or accidental disclosure. Digital records are transmitted via modern Transport Layer Security encryption protocols and stored on hardened servers featuring multi-factor authentication and role-restricted access rights. Only credentialed concierge personnel and authorized master tailoring directors possessing verified operational need are permitted access to private fitting consultation records.

6. Client Privacy Rights

Under applicable federal and state data privacy legislation, including the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act and California Consumer Privacy Act, you maintain the legal entitlement to access, inspect, rectify, or request permanent deletion of your stored contact records. If you desire to review your personal records or withdraw communication consent, you may transmit a formal written demand to our administrative concierge. We will review and fulfill verified data subject requests within thirty calendar days without cost.

7. Strict Non-Monetization Policy

Overcoat Prism maintains an ethical operational posture that strictly prohibits the monetization, sale, trade, or commercial leasing of client contact details to external marketing agencies, commercial broker networks, or unrelated merchant entities. We disclose client details solely to vetted technical service providers under binding confidentiality obligations, or when formally compelled by lawful governmental subpoenas, valid judicial search warrants, or mandatory statutory requirements under United States federal jurisdiction.

8. Administrative Inquiries & Transparency

Should you have questions, detailed inquiries, clarifications, or feedback concerning the contents of this Institutional Privacy Policy, we welcome your direct communication with our administrative team. We remain deeply committed to fostering open transparency, tailoring excellence, and lasting mutual trust with every patron who engages with our digital portal, reviews our outerwear archives, or reserves fitting experiences at our Chicago Magnificent Mile salon rooms.

9. Institutional Contact Coordinates

Please direct all formal inquiries regarding this privacy framework to Overcoat Prism Bespoke Outerwear LLC, located at 980 N Michigan Avenue, Suite 1600, Chicago, IL 60611, United States. For immediate verbal consultations regarding digital security safeguards or salon reservation records, you may contact our concierge by telephone at +1-888-592-3104 or transmit electronic inquiries to concierge@overcoatprism.com. We remain dedicated to serving our patrons with integrity and bespoke craftsmanship excellence.
