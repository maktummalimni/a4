2. Handcrafted Bespoke Outerwear Standards

Overcoat Prism designs and hand-constructs bespoke outerwear featuring traditional horsehair canvassing, hand-padded lapels, and heavy British and Italian woolens. Because each coat is individually cut and stitched by hand, slight natural variations in wool grain texture, hand-stitch spacing, and horn button color striations reflect authentic artisanal craftsmanship. Such natural artisan variations do not constitute tailoring defects or breach of contract under any commercial warranties.

3. Fitting Reservations & Consultation Protocols

Because our atelier prepares custom pattern drafts, reserves dedicated cutting table hours, and prepares cloth swatches specifically for scheduled appointments, private bespoke fitting sessions require a credit card guarantee. Your appointment is not legally confirmed until our concierge reviews your request and issues an authenticated reservation card by email. Patrons must arrive promptly at their scheduled Chicago hour to ensure adequate consultation time with our master cutter.

4. Cancellation Notice & Scheduling

Patrons seeking to reschedule or cancel a confirmed bespoke fitting must provide written or verbal notice to our concierge at least forty-eight hours prior to the scheduled hour. Because late cancellations prevent our artisans from offering high-demand cutting bench slots to fellow clients on our waiting roster, failure to attend without timely notice may incur late cancellation fees as outlined in our cancellation policy. We appreciate client cooperation in preserving our disciplined tailoring calendar.
