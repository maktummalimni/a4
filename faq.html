Bespoke Outerwear FAQ

Everything you need to know regarding in-person fittings, canvas construction, cloth selection, and lifelong maintenance of your handcrafted coat.

Common Questions

Clarity before your first fitting session

Whether you are commissioning your first bespoke heavy overcoat or adding a specialized cashmere storm coat to your wardrobe, we believe in complete operational transparency.

Review our core tailoring protocols below or speak directly with our concierge team at +1-888-592-3104.

1. The Bespoke Fitting Process

How many fittings are required for a bespoke overcoat?

A full bespoke overcoat typically requires three fitting stages: the initial anatomical measurement and cloth selection, the skeleton baste fitting where the raw canvas and wool are evaluated on your body, and the forward fitting where sleeves and hem lengths are perfected.

What is the typical turnaround time from commission to delivery?

Because each garment involves over fifty hours of hand needlework, turnaround averages six to eight weeks. For urgent winter travel requirements, expedited salon scheduling can occasionally be arranged with our head cutter.

2. Construction & Canvas Materials

What is the advantage of full floating horsehair canvas?

Commercial coats use synthetic glue that stiffens cloth unnaturally and bubbles over time. Our floating horsehair canvas breathes with your movements, rolls softly across the lapels, and gradually molds to your torso's contours, becoming more comfortable with every winter worn.

Where do your overcoat fabrics originate?

We source heavy woolens, meltons, tweeds, and cashmeres exclusively from historic heritage mills in Yorkshire, the Scottish Borders, and Northern Italy, guaranteeing exceptional density, weather resistance, and natural warmth.

3. Consultations & Rescheduling

Is the consultation deposit credited toward the finished coat?

Yes, absolutely. The consultation booking deposit is credited 100% toward the total balance of your commissioned bespoke overcoat.

How can I reschedule or cancel my fitting appointment?

Appointments may be rescheduled or cancelled without fee up to 48 hours prior to your session by emailing concierge@overcoatprism.com or telephoning our desk. Inside 48 hours, late rescheduling provisions apply in accordance with our Cancellation Policy.
