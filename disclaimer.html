2. Fabric Weight & Performance Benchmarks

Overcoat Prism expressly disclaims all legal liability for inadvertent typographical errors, computational discrepancies, or unintentional omissions that may occur across our digital archives. Patrons are encouraged to verify fabric performance directly with our Chicago bench staff.

3. Wool Garment Care & Natural Fiber Notices

Bespoke wool and cashmere outerwear garments are artisanal tailored creations constructed from natural organic fibers sensitive to improper storage, moth damage, excessive moisture exposure, and harsh dry cleaning solvents. While our coats feature reinforced seams and water-repellent weaves, owners must exercise proper care, including using wide cedar hangers, natural bristle brushing, and seasonal breathable storage. Nothing published on this website constitutes an unconditional guarantee against damage resulting from improper home care or environmental neglect.
